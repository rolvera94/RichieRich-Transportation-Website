## Rules for every image
- **No logos, no lettering, no numbers, no license plates** on the trucks or
  anywhere else. AI makes fake logos and garbled text. Our only logo is the gold
  RR mark, and the website adds it itself.
- **No people**, or at most a small figure far away with no face visible.
- **Wide (landscape)**, at least **2400 px wide** for the hero, 1600 px for the rest.
- Photo-realistic, not illustrated. Vary the time of day across the set
  (midday, morning, dusk) so the page doesn't read as all sunsets.
- Texas setting: Houston-area highways, East Texas pines, big sky. No cowboy
  clichés.

## Which AI tool
- **Adobe Firefly** (firefly.adobe.com) is the best pick for a business: its
  images are licensed for commercial use. Free monthly credits.
- **ChatGPT**, **Google Gemini**, or **Microsoft Designer / Bing Image Creator**
  also work.
- **Tip:** in ChatGPT or Gemini you can upload our real truck photo and say
  *"same truck, same color and grille, but remove every logo, number, and plate,
  and put it on a Texas highway at sunset."* That's the fastest way to get
  images that look like **our** trucks.
